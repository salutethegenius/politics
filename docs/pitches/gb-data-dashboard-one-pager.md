## The Gap

The Grand Bahama Port Authority publishes **Invest Grand Bahama** — a public-facing map of investment, development, and business activity inside the Port Area.

The **Ministry for Grand Bahama** — whose own stated portfolio includes *"Community Engagement and Empowerment"* and *"Promotion of Employment and Entrepreneurial Opportunities in Grand Bahama"* — has no equivalent. There is no public dashboard tracking GB investment, employment, or program activity outside the Port Area, and no visible way for residents, businesses, or the Ministry itself to see progress against its own mandate in one place.

## Why Now

A February 27, 2026 arbitral ruling on the Hawksbill Creek Agreement confirmed that GBPA must continue making annual payments to the Government and — more importantly — rejected GBPA's claim to *exclusive* administrative authority over Freeport, affirming the Government's continuing legislative and regulatory authority over the Port Area. This is the first time in decades that the Government's institutional footprint in Freeport has this much fresh, public legitimacy. A government-owned GB data layer is timely, not redundant.
